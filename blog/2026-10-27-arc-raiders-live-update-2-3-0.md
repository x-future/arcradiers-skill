# ARC Raiders Live Update 2.3.0

Embark's October 27, 2026 update adds the Noxious Set and the Souls Offering player project, then follows with a broad pass of gameplay, map, UI, stability and visual fixes. This page condenses the official notes into a build-planning reference.

## Quick facts

- Release date: October 27, 2026
- New project: Souls Offering
- Notable balance change: Heavy Shield base charge increased from 80 to 90
- Source: [Live Update 2.3.0](https://arcraiders.com/news/live-update-2-3-0)

## Souls Offering and Noxious Set

The new Souls Offering project asks Raiders to remember fallen players and the marks they left on the fight against ARC. Each offering is unique, with the project framed as a communal pause across the four contradas of Toledo.

The update also introduces the Noxious Set. The official announcement includes new store and Souls Offering images, which are shown below for reference.

## ARC, Frigate and animation fixes

- Emperor loot is now distributed with lower-tier containers near the entrance and better rewards deeper inside.
- Dead rolling enemies no longer leave tracks, and ARC movement near map borders is improved.
- Vaporizer debris now respects its weight instead of flying or jittering.
- New Frigate hatches make puzzle progress easier to read; collision gaps that allowed hiding inside geometry or getting stuck on the wings are fixed.
- Bull Rush's hit camera animation received a small adjustment, and reload magazines now eject correctly on Bettina, Bobcat and Kettle.

## Gameplay and equipment changes

The headline equipment change is a Heavy Shield base charge increase from 80 to 90. The developer note says the goal is a small survivability bump while keeping a clear difference from the Medium Shield.

Other gameplay changes include more accurate Frigate pings, broader exploit prevention in Emperor locked rooms, stricter barricade placement, and killbox adjustments in the Pendola Pass Emperor. Heavy landings now trigger correctly during dodge rolls, including cases that previously avoided fall damage. Scrappy also stays correctly positioned in the Outpost.

### Items

- Mines can no longer float when placed on dismantling barricades, and Flame Spray fire no longer passes through walls.
- Flame Spray damage rises to 13 per second from 10, while its durability drain is reduced by 50%.
- Firefly Burner now ignites on impact, leaves a fire trail, and explodes after about three seconds.
- Adrenaline Shot works at max stamina and refreshes its duration rather than stacking.
- Furniture design drop rates are corrected, and several weapon blueprints can now drop in any major map condition.
- Emperor Key blueprint odds from Redirection pods are reduced, with ARC parts and high-tier ARC loot added as compensation.

### Grappling Hook, camera and zipline

Grappling Hook reliability is improved inside the Matriarch shield, around breakable debris, and when a rope wraps around an obstacle. Tether items now affect players swinging from a hook, and several extreme-launch and stuck-animation cases are fixed.

The advanced camera gains Color Push and Black & White filters. Classic Film grain is refined and Basic Camera depth-of-field blur is reduced. Zipline entry now lifts the character correctly, and long-distance preview cables no longer disappear.

## Map, performance and stability

Pendola Pass receives the largest collision pass, covering the fallen Emperor, gondola extraction, shelter chairs, Storm Hut containers and an ammo box. Spaceport and Stella Montis also get fixes for locations where players could become stuck.

FSR is updated to 4.1.1. Xbox Series S audio memory use is reduced, Frigate memory and crash behavior are improved, a post-round tunnel failsafe was added, and an AMD RX 7000 ray-tracing crash workaround is included.

## UI and quality-of-life updates

The Expedition Vault is being disabled; purchased slots are compensated with Bronze Statuettes and stored items move to the stash. Other UI changes include correct Raider Tool and melee controller hints, widescreen camera HUD coverage, better weapon mod and stencil information on the End of Round screen, and a camera bob option in Gameplay settings.

Codex scrolling, Bilguun's Hideout unlocks, Outpost furniture framing, Reward Pass layout, Weapon Amplification tooltips, Project notification dots and Workshop navigation all receive targeted fixes or polish.

## Known issues

The official list still includes the Raider Tool default-skin issue, incomplete quest objectives, lost quest-item blockers, Xbox instability, ARC Reclaimer docking and vaulting problems, oversized weapon models, Grappling Hook bounce behavior, and a Railroad stuck spot on Pendola Pass. Check the [official notes](https://arcraiders.com/news/live-update-2-3-0) before treating any of these as resolved.
