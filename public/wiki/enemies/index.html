ARC Machine Index

Official descriptions are condensed into the behavior signal a Raider needs during an encounter.

Ground ARC / Heavy

Bastion

An armored ground machine with target-switch audio cues and vulnerable leg joints or weak spots.

Open entry
Interior ambusher

Tick

A small eight-legged ARC that clings to walls and ceilings before jumping an unaware Raider.

Flying scout

Snitch

Aerial surveillance ARC that calls reinforcements after spotting suspicious activity.

Ground assault

Leaper

A large patrol machine defined by a destructive leap attack and sudden gap-closing pressure.

Indirect fire

Bombardier

Works with Spotter drones to designate Raiders for lethal mortar attacks.

Boss ARC

Queen

An enormous machine that defends the Harvester and can punish unprepared groups with stomp pressure.

Boss ARC

Matriarch

An imposing machine introduced with Stella Montis and encountered under a specific map condition.

Close quarters

Shredder

A fast ARC introduced at Stella Montis for dangerous interior and close-range encounters.

Flying hunter

Firefly

A persistent flying flame threat that can chase Raiders into cover instead of holding distance.

Focused predator

Comet

A rolling explosive ARC with armor plates, an exposed inner weak point and high burst threat.

Riven Tides

ARC Turbine

A floating ARC introduced with Riven Tides whose loot value was increased in Live Update 1.36.0.

Flying ARC

Wasp

One of the Rust Belt flying ARC types and a recurring Feat target in official game examples.

Threat Classes

Use size only as a first clue; detection behavior and attack pattern are often more important.

ClassExamplesPrimary response
Detection and pursuitSnitch, Firefly, WaspBreak line of sight, remove surveillance quickly and avoid letting an alert escalate.
Ambush and close pressureTick, Leaper, Shredder, CometPreserve movement space and learn the cue that precedes the machine's committed attack.
Armored and indirect fireBastion, Bombardier, RocketeerFight from hard cover, attack vulnerable components and avoid static open trades.
Boss-scale ARCQueen, MatriarchTreat the encounter as an objective requiring preparation, ammunition depth and an exit plan.
